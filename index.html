✦ What is Canva Fest?

Design. Create. Inspire.

A one-of-a-kind creative design competition and workshop for students to explore and master Canva.

Your Canvas, Your Rules

Canva Fest is a creative playground where students get to explore the power of visual design using Canva — the world's favorite design platform. Whether you're a beginner or a design pro, this is your chance to shine.

From stunning posters to eye-catching presentations, participants will compete in exciting design challenges that test creativity, aesthetic sense, and speed. Winners walk away with recognition, certificates, and bragging rights!
