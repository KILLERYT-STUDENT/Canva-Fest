Rules & Guidelines

Read carefully before participating in Canva Fest 2026

📜

Competition Rules

01

Team Size

Each team will consist of 2 students. Solo entries are not allowed.

02

Total Teams

Maximum 15 teams will be allowed to participate. First come, first served.

03

Time Duration

Each team gets 1 hour to complete their design. Manage your time wisely.

04

Topic

The design topic will be revealed at the spot on the day of the event. Come prepared for anything!

05

Tools & Resources

All tools and resources on Canva are allowed. Exception: Creating the poster/design using AI is strictly prohibited.

06

Independent Work

Each team must work independently. No collaboration between teams during the event.

07

No Copying

Sharing, discussing, or copying ideas from another team leads to immediate disqualification.

08

Judge's Decision

The judges' decisions will be final. No arguments or objections will be entertained.

09

Time Management

Participants must value their time. Design must be submitted before the time ends. Late submissions will not be accepted.

10

Appropriate Content

Students must ensure they use appropriate and safe words & images in their design. Inappropriate content leads to disqualification.

11

Design Usage Rights

The school reserves the right to use submitted designs for promotional/advertising purposes.

📱

Gadgets & Devices

✅ Phones and Laptops are allowed for the competition.

⚠️ If anything happens to your gadgets during the event, the school will not be held responsible. Bring your devices at your own risk.
